    No IPTV provider yet?

    Try ChannelBin first with free, legal channels from M3UPT. M3UPT is a public list of free and open channels that the broadcasters themselves stream online. Note that ChannelBin doesn't control these channels or this playlist... hopefully it doesn't break and create issues in the future. :)

    The button adds:

    • An account named "{{ o.account_name }}" that reads M3UPT's playlist and program listings: about a thousand channels, mostly Portuguese TV and radio, with English-language news among them. Its first sync starts straight away.
    • {{ o.guide_count }} English-language channels in your TV Guide: {{ o.guide }}.
    • A channel group, "{{ o.group_name }}", holding two Bloomberg TV feeds that share one program listing, which is what a channel group is for.
    • A health check on {{ o.test_channel }}.

    Everything it adds is marked as an example. After they're added you'll have an option to delete the examples which cleans everything up and takes it all out in one step. If M3UPT turns out to be all you want, you will also have an option to keep the examples if it turns out you want to keep the M3UPT items that are added.

    Finding things: two kinds of search

    Search asks first what you want to search, and the two answers find different things.

    Search Channels

    One row per channel. It answers "which of my channels is this?" Use it to find a channel by name, then add it to the TV Guide, test it, or put it in a group. It looks at channel names unless you tell it otherwise.

    Search Channels {{ icon('forward') }}

    Search Programs (EPG)

    One row per showing, earliest first, from your accounts' program listings. It answers "when is this on, and on which channel?" Type a show, a team or a word from a description, and every showing it finds has its time and a Record button. An auto-record rule is one of these searches, saved.

    Search Programs (EPG) {{ icon('forward') }}

    Click into the search box to see Search in, which picks what your words are matched against: channel name, category and ids, or program title, episode and description. On Search Channels a program field matches only what each channel is showing right now. All words or Any word, beside the box, says whether every word has to match. + Filter narrows the results by account, category, health, channel group, tag or status, and on programs by when, day and time of day. Saved searches keeps a search under a name.
