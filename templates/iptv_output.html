IPTV Output {{ beta_mark('IPTV Output') }}

{% call page_about() %}

An IPTV player (like TiviMate) can connect to ChannelBin as if it were an IPTV provider. Everything it plays still comes from your own IPTV provider. What the player gets is your TV Guide: the same channels and channel groups, in the same order, with the same listings. A group plays as one channel and moves to another of its channels if the one playing dies.

This page is where you find the details you would enter into your IPTV player, see and manage what channels and categories are offered, see who is watching, and more.

Note: A recording always wins. If ChannelBin needs the connection to your IPTV provider to complete a recording and someone is streaming from that account, the recording takes the connection. A group or sports channel moves to another of its channels that has a connection free; anything else, or one with nowhere to go, is cut so the recording can run.

Accounts it may play from

While someone watches, the play uses one connection of one of your IPTV accounts, and a recording that needs that connection takes it. Switch an account off to keep the IPTV output away from it altogether: a channel on it leaves the output, and a group plays from its channels on the other accounts.

{% if mixed %}

In mixed mode a channel that is not a group or a sports channel plays straight from your IPTV provider, and ChannelBin cannot see whether an IPTV player is still watching it, so it cannot take that connection back when a recording needs it. If a recording starts on an account whose connections are all in use, your IPTV provider refuses one of the two. To keep that from happening, tell recordings to leave a connection alone on the accounts players watch from. A group or sports channel playing through ChannelBin uses one of those connections first, so the same player is never counted twice. "None" is how it starts: recordings may use every connection. Leaving all of them alone means nothing records from that account. Whether the output may use an account at all is the switch, not this.

{% elif redirect %}

In redirect mode ChannelBin cannot see whether an IPTV player is still watching, so it cannot take a connection back when a recording needs it. If a recording starts on an account whose connections are all in use, your IPTV provider refuses one of the two. To keep that from happening, tell recordings to leave a connection alone on the accounts players watch from. "None" is how it starts: recordings may use every connection, and the output still plays from the account. Leaving all of them alone means nothing records from that account. Whether the output may use an account at all is the switch, not this.
