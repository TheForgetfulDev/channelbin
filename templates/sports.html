{% call page_about() %}

Finding a live game, match, or race on a sports channel can often be harder than it should be. The same event is listed again and again with nothing to say which airing is live, a guide's time can be wrong, and many event channels have no real listings at all, only a name such as "NFL | 1pm Cowboys at Colts". Sports Schedules gets the real schedule of the leagues and teams you follow from TheSportsDB (who is playing, where, and when) and checks each event against your guide. You can then see which channel an event is actually on, and which listing is the live one and which is a replay.

Add a whole league, or a specific team you follow, and that league's page lists its events with every channel carrying each one, whether it is the live airing or a replay, and why each listing matched. If one is wrong, mark it a Wrong match and it stops counting. An event channel with no listings is read by its name.

Once you've added a league or team, an auto-record rule can use it to record that league's or team's events automatically. You can also include TheSportsDB fields in filename templates: fields such as {sports:league} and {sports:event} are filled in when the file is written.

You can configure TheSportsDB options, such as how far apart calls are, how far ahead a schedule reaches, and how long past events are kept, in Settings.
