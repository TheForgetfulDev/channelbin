A channel group bundles identical feeds of one channel. Turn on Recording for the members you would record from and the group can be a single TV Guide row: recordings start on the healthiest enabled member and fail over to the next-best one if it dies. A health check is a schedule the group carries, testing its members to confirm they still play — a group can carry more than one, and a group with no member enabled for recording is simply a health check.
