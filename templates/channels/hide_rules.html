Take channels out of your way without deleting them - a blanket rule over a provider category or a channel name, on top of hiding one channel at a time from its own page. A hidden channel leaves search, the pickers and the counts, keeps its detail page, and stops having EPG imported for it. A channel in your TV Guide or a channel group stays visible until you remove it from there, no matter what a rule says.
